Why Childcare CHEERO?

Early learning should build more than academic skills.

Children are learning how to communicate, discovering who they are, learning kindness, developing confidence, building relationships, and beginning to understand the world around them. That's why Childcare CHEERO combines early childhood learning with faith and character development.

Christ-Centered Learning

Faith. Purpose. Kindness. Character.

Prepare children academically while also planting seeds that can grow with them.

Learning That Grows With Them

From infancy through age 5.
